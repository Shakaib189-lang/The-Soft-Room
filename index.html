The eleventh stitch

Named after the one everyone else skips.

In upholstery, ten stitches per inch is the standard. Eleven takes longer and costs more thread — and it is the difference between a seam that lasts two years and one that lasts twelve.

That extra stitch is the whole idea behind this shop. It is in the reinforced mattress edges, the double-turned hem on our sheets, and the corner ties inside every duvet cover.

  • Reinforced perimeter stitching on every mattress
  • Foam density printed on the label, not hidden
  • Covers that unzip and go in your washing machine
